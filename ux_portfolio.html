Philips · UX Research Intern · Bothell, WA

Conducted end-to-end user research across multiple product streams — from mobile app usability to hardware validation — to surface actionable insights for Philips' Oral Healthcare division. Research directly informed 2024 digital product strategy and contributed to urgent quality improvement actions.

Research process

01
Discovery & literature review Synthesized internal and external literature to establish the research landscape for entry-level oral healthcare customers. Delivered strategic insights to the global Oral Healthcare community, generating additional research questions for the product value stream.

Quantitative validation analysis Analyzed 1,000+ data points from toothbrush validation studies using Excel to determine UI pain points and user preferences. Findings were presented directly to the Product Research Center director, informing the 2024 digital product roadmap.
03

05
Reporting & communication Produced monthly and quarterly user research reports using standardized slide deck templates, delivering translated findings and recommendations to global stakeholders across the Oral Healthcare division.

Social participation & life satisfaction in TBI patients: the moderating role of binge drinking

Master's Thesis · Seattle Pacific University

Examined how binge drinking moderates the relationship between social participation and life satisfaction in young adults (18–25) two years post-TBI. Used the TBI Model Systems National Database and validated psychometric instruments (PART-O, SWLS) to conduct moderation analysis on a large secondary dataset.

Findings revealed a protective effects of social engagement during TBI recovery — with direct implications for clinical intervention design and program targeting.
